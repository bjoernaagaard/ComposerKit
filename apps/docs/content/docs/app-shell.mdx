## Keyboard

The title bar tabs follow the [WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices tabs pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/): Left and Right move to the previous and next tab and open it, and Home and End go to the first and last. The mode toggle is two [toggle buttons](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/button/), each reached with Tab and pressed with Enter or Space.
